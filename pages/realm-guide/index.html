How Three Realms Works

Three Realms Adventures is an original tabletop roleplaying experience built around distinct realms, guided character creation, campaign play, and expandable adventure content.

Players & Realm Masters

Players create heroes and make choices within the story. The Realm Master prepares the world, presents challenges, guides encounters, and brings each campaign to life.
